When 3-Axis Programming Is the Right Fit

Three-axis machining covers most milling work: the tool moves in X, Y and Z while the part stays fixed in the setup. It is simple to fixture, quick to program and economical to run, which is why it suits plates, brackets, covers, fixtures and many mould inserts.

Parts with features on several faces are done in multiple setups. We plan those setups so datum features carry across and the number of re-clamps stays low.

  • 2.5D work: facing, pockets, profiles, slots, drilling and tapping.
  • 3D surfacing with parallel, contour and rest-machining strategies.
  • Sensible stepdowns and stepovers for the tool and material.
  • Setup sheets and tool lists with the program.

FAQ

Common Questions

What is 3-axis CAM programming?

It is programming where the cutting tool moves along the X, Y and Z axes. It suits most milling work such as pockets, profiles, holes and 3D surfaces.

When should I choose 4-axis or 5-axis instead of 3-axis?

If a part needs features on several sides or around a cylinder, or has undercuts and complex contours, a rotary axis can cut setups and improve results. We can advise from your part model.

Do you offer 3-axis programming in Coimbatore?

Yes. We provide 3-axis Mastercam programming from Coimbatore for job shops and manufacturers.

Can you program 3D surfaces and moulds?

Yes. We use roughing, rest-machining and finishing strategies for 3D surfaces, with the surface finish and tool reach taken into account.
