CAD Modelling Built for Manufacturing

Have an idea but no 3D file? We create CAD models and drawings from sketches, reference photos or a physical part. Designs are made with the manufacturing process in mind — CNC machining or 3D printing — so the model works on the machine, not just on screen.

Because we also program CNC machines, our designs take machining reality into account: tool access, setups and tolerances.

  • 3D modelling in SolidWorks, CATIA and Fusion 360.
  • 2D drawings and drafting for manufacturing.
  • Reverse engineering of existing physical parts.
  • Design for Additive Manufacturing (DFAM) for 3D printed parts.

FAQ

Common Questions

What CAD software do you use?

We work with SolidWorks, CATIA and Fusion 360, depending on the part and manufacturing target.

Can you design a part from just a rough sketch?

Yes. We turn sketches, hand drawings or verbal concepts into detailed, manufacturable 3D models.

Do you do reverse engineering of existing parts?

Yes. We create accurate CAD models from existing physical parts, useful when drawings are missing.

Do you provide 2D drawings as well as 3D models?

Yes. We provide both 3D models and 2D manufacturing drawings.

What is Design for Additive Manufacturing (DFAM)?

DFAM is designing parts specifically for 3D printing, considering print orientation, supports and material behaviour for the best strength and finish.
