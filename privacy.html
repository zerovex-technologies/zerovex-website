Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry or quote request, we collect the contact details and project information you provide. A quote request can also include a CAD or 3D design file.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote, communicate about the requested work, and maintain service records where required.

File uploads and third parties

Quote files and request details are transmitted to the form and file-processing services used to operate this website. Do not upload confidential, export-controlled, or personal data unless you are authorised to share it. We recommend removing unnecessary sensitive metadata before upload.

Retention and your choices

We retain enquiry information only for as long as needed to respond, provide services, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes. You may request access, correction, or deletion of your information by contacting us.

Contact

For privacy requests, email info@zerovex.in.
