Programming for Vertical Machining Centres

A vertical machining centre is the workhorse of most job shops, and the quality of its programs decides cycle time, tool life and surface finish. We program VMC parts in Mastercam with the machine, vise or fixture, and tool list in mind, so the program matches how the part will actually be held and cut.

That covers plates, brackets, housings, fixtures, moulds and dies, from simple 2.5D work to 3D surfacing, and VMCs with a rotary or trunnion table for 4-axis and 5-axis jobs.

  • Facing, pocketing, profiling, drilling, tapping and 3D surfacing.
  • Roughing strategies that protect tool life, with finishing passes for surface finish.
  • Work offsets, tool numbers and tool lists organised for the operator.
  • VMCs with 4th-axis or 5-axis tables supported.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do you provide VMC programming services in Coimbatore?

Yes. We provide VMC programming from Coimbatore, delivering Mastercam toolpaths as G-code for your machine and controller.

Can you program VMCs with a 4th or 5th axis?

Yes. We program VMCs fitted with rotary or trunnion tables, including 4-axis indexing and 5-axis jobs.

What information do you need to program a VMC part?

The part model or drawing, material, machine make and model, controller, work-holding, and the tools you have available.

Can you reduce my cycle time on an existing VMC program?

We can review the part and rework toolpaths with better roughing and finishing strategies. The gain depends on the part, tooling and machine, so we assess each job before promising a result.
