Why Post Processors Matter

A post processor translates your CAM toolpaths into the exact G-Code dialect your machine controller understands. When it's mismatched, wrong, or outdated, you get program crashes, wrong tool offsets, incorrect canned cycles, or scrapped parts. Most shops discover this the hard way, right when a new machine arrives or production is on the line.

  • Fix existing post processors throwing errors or wrong output.
  • Build new post processors from scratch for any CAM software.
  • Adapt existing posts for a new machine or controller upgrade.
